spreadthewiki

One Wikipedia article a day.

spreadthewiki is a YouTube Shorts channel. Each video explains one English Wikipedia article in under a minute, in original words, with flat illustrations and AI narration.

Article text is used under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 license (CC BY-SA 4.0). Every video description names the article and links the exact revision that was used.

The videos are produced by a private automation operated by the channel owner. Finished videos are uploaded to the owner's own YouTube channel as private; the owner reviews and approves every video before it is published.
